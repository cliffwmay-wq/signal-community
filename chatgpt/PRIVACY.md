# Signal privacy and provenance

Signal's packaged instructions and optional Python inspector perform no telemetry, analytics, account creation, networking, or automatic document modification. The inspector reports exact artifacts from a local UTF-8 text file; it does not rewrite the file.

Text pasted into ChatGPT, attached, or made available through authorized tools is handled by that service according to the user's account and workspace settings. Signal cannot promise zero retention or confidentiality beyond those settings. Share only writing you are allowed to use, and remove unnecessary personal or client details before submitting samples.

Voice DNA profiles are optional, human-readable, and controlled by the author. A profile is a draft until the author reviews it. Signal does not save raw samples or a profile by default. If the user asks to save a profile, place it in their chosen private location, not the plugin or a public repository.

Signal does not identify AI authorship, remove statistical text watermarks, or guarantee any detector result. It does not silently remove required AI-use disclosures, licensing notes, attribution, or provenance records.
