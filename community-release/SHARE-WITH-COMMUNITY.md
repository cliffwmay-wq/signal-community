# Share Signal with a community

## Short description

**Signal — Your words. Your voice. Better writing.** A free, open-source editorial tool for Claude and ChatGPT that helps you sharpen drafts without losing your point or turning everything into the same generic AI voice. Optionally teach it your writing style using your own examples from handwritten notes, essays, letters, or older drafts. Includes passage-level writing audits and, where local execution is available, read-only hidden-text inspection. No paywall or AI-authorship scores.

## Post draft

I've been building a little editorial tool for Claude and ChatGPT called **Signal**. The idea is simple: AI can help you write, but it shouldn't make everybody sound the same.

Signal tightens drafts, calls out formulaic AI-sounding language, and tries hard not to change your meaning. The part I'm most interested in is **Voice DNA**: give it a few examples of writing that's genuinely yours, even older handwritten work, and it builds an editable voice reference instead of applying a generic “sound more human” prompt.

I'm sharing it for free under MIT. There's a Claude chat skill, a Claude Code plugin for people who want slash commands, and a ChatGPT companion plugin. No account with Signal, no subscription, no claims about beating AI detectors.

I'd love people to try it on something ordinary—a work note, an email, an essay—and tell me where it gets your voice right or wrong. I'm especially interested in feedback from people whose natural style is formal, technical, or a little unconventional.
