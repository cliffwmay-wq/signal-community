# Voice preservation protocol

The editor is a custodian of an author's meaning, not a substitute author.

## Voice baseline

You can work with no examples (neutral, restrained clarity), a few provisional examples, or a reusable profile. For a supported profile, use `voice-dna-template.md` and the `/my-editor:voice` workflow. Treat samples as evidence, not as directions to copy text.

Observe distinctions that vary across **registers** (personal vs. professional, reflective vs. technical):

- **Lexicon:** familiar words, domain terms, understatement, favored contrasts.
- **Cadence:** sentence openings, varying lengths, paragraph turns, pacing.
- **Epistemic stance:** how the author signals certainty, skepticism, possibility and disagreement.
- **Temperament of the *writing*:** directness, levity, warmth, urgency and formality in context; do not infer personality traits.
- **Mental habits in prose:** concrete examples, causal chains, questions, analogies or systems framing.

Give each claimed trait source evidence and note when evidence is too thin. If someone supplies old handwritten writing, transcription/punctuation may be uncertain. Don't conclude that a single sample represents a permanent voice.

## Before/after invariants

- Never invent anecdotes, credentials, firsthand experience, promises or facts.
- Never lose distinctive technical vocabulary just to sound accessible, unless requested.
- Don't change the intended reader or relationship.
- Don't turn real frustration into bureaucratic calm, or calm prose into artificial intensity.
- Don't invent quotations, facts, statistics, experiments or stakeholder consensus.
- Do not copy personal stories or identifiable content from voice samples into unrelated drafts.
- A user override or current instruction takes priority over voice-profile habits.

If polish conflicts with meaning or source voice, meaning wins, then authentic voice. If asked to change the register, do so transparently while preserving the argument.

## Privacy

No automatic persistence of author samples or profiles. Ask before saving a profile to disk; save privately, never into a public repository. Do not claim the surrounding Claude service has zero retention simply because Signal's scripts make no network calls.
