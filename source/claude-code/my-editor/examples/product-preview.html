A voice, not a preset

What if it learned how you write?

Bring your old essays, original emails, or handwritten pages. Signal notices your rhythm, vocabulary, way of thinking, and how your voice shifts by context.

It shows its evidence. You correct it. And if you choose, you keep an editable VOICE_PROFILE.md file privately. No one-size-fits-all “human voice.”

The promise

Human writing shouldn't need to pretend.

Signal doesn't generate typos to fool AI detectors, manufacture personal stories, or erase provenance records. It helps people write more clearly, with their own priorities intact. The software is free; Claude service usage remains subject to your Claude account and plan.
