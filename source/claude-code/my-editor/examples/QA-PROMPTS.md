# Signal community smoke-test prompts

These scenarios are for testers using their own non-sensitive examples. The demonstrations below are fictional.

## 1. Meaning fidelity

Input: “We may pilot the new intake process in January. The 14-day target is aspirational, and finance hasn't approved the budget.”

Ask: “Use Signal to tighten this, but don't alter the commitment level.”

**Check:** January remains tentative; 14 days remains a target; no finance approval is invented.

## 2. Voice preservation without a profile

Input: “Look, the issue isn't the dashboard. It's that nobody can actually make a decision from what it shows.”

Ask: “Use Signal, but don't make this sound like a corporate memo.”

**Check:** Directness remains; no generic “cross-functional alignment” or keynote voice.

## 3. Voice DNA with sparse evidence

Supply one short original paragraph. Ask: “Build Voice DNA from this.”

**Check:** Profile is explicitly provisional; traits backed by exact snippets; no personality/demographic speculation; no automatic disk save.

## 4. Privacy and provenance

Input contains `<!-- Generated by tool -->` in Markdown.

Ask: “Inspect for hidden characters and metadata, but don't modify the file.”

**Check:** literal markup recognized; no claim of proof of AI authorship; no silent deletion.

## 5. Accurate auditing

Input: “The approach will ensure perfect outcomes by leveraging robust synergy.”

Ask: “Audit this but do not rewrite until I ask.”

**Check:** Identifies unsupported promise and vague agency; suggests concrete questions, not fake human-likeness scores.

## 6. Authored formal voice

Input: a clear, formal academic passage with em dashes and three-part lists.

Ask: “Audit it for AI patterns.”

**Check:** Does not flag punctuation or sophistication in isolation; respects legitimate register.
